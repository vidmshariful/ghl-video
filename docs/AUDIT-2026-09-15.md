# Platform audit, 15 September 2026

Written the evening after go-live, for Shariful. Every corner was looked
at: six independent code reviews (security and auth, the money path, the
HighLevel mirror, the portal and admin screens, crons and email,
configuration and operations), and the live platform itself, read-only:
all 27 public pages, the Stripe account and webhook, the production
database's consistency, the HighLevel sub-account, the crons, the gates,
and every admin and portal screen opened in a browser as the owner and as
the demo client. Findings marked "verified" were confirmed by reading the
code or the live data myself; the rest carry the reviewer's evidence.

Nothing was changed. The fixes below wait for your yes.

---

## 1. Fix before tomorrow morning

### 1.1 The first morning sweep would nudge clients about old things (verified)

The morning sweep never ran before today (its cron path was redirected).
Its first real run is tomorrow, 16 September, 09:00 UTC. Its queries have
no date bounds and an empty ledger, so against production it would send:

- **Brief reminders to four clients, three of them wrong.** Keith and
  Small Screen Producer have packs in review, Vini's video is delivered,
  yet each would be told "nothing can start until your branding arrives"
  because the brief form was never used on those orders. Only one (paid
  25 August, still no brief) is a real reminder. Cause: the query has no
  stage filter (`app/api/cron/chase/route.ts:203-221`).
- **Approval nudges with large day counts**: Vini (a video waiting since
  22 August), Keith (4 videos since 4 September), Small Screen Producer
  (5), Scott (5), plus three custom stations sitting with the client
  (two of Chase's, one of Evoke's). "Waiting on you for 24 days" is a
  true sentence, but it is the first thing those clients would hear from
  the sweep.
- **Review requests to four clients** whose first job finished more than
  two days ago: Vini, Chase, Evoke, Keith. This is the rule you chose,
  but note it includes the retainer partner.
- The internal demo account is skipped in some loops and not others
  (`chase/route.ts`, the project, format, editing and digest loops).

Two ways to handle it, your call: pause the sweep (one line in
`vercel.json`) until the fixes below ship, or ship the fixes tonight with a
start line so nothing older than fourteen days is chased. Either way the
brief reminder needs its stage filter, the internal account needs skipping
everywhere, and a bounced address should not be chased again (the ledger
counts only `sent`).

### 1.2 Anyone can create a client's portal login before they buy (verified)

Checkout creates the portal login at the "finalize" step, before any money
moves, with the password the caller typed (`app/api/checkout/finalize/
route.ts:240`, `lib/checkout/account.ts:50`). A login is made confirmed,
with no mailbox check. So anyone who knows a prospect's email can mint an
intent, call finalize with that email and a password of their choosing,
and own the account; when the real client later buys, their own password
is discarded as "already registered" and the attacker signs in to their
orders, videos, invoices and brand kit. Two smaller holes compound it:

- **An unpaid order is a key to an existing client's brand kit
  (verified).** The intake page is gated only by the order id, which
  finalize hands back. Its GET returns the client's brand name, colours,
  notes and a signed logo link; its POST overwrites them
  (`app/api/intake/[orderId]/route.ts:106`, `:321`).
- **Ownership checks treat `_` and `%` in the signed-in email as
  wildcards (verified).** Twenty-three portal routes pass the email
  straight into `ilike` (`app/api/portal/invoices/route.ts:37` and
  siblings). A login `j_hn@x.com` matches `john@x.com`.

Fix, contained to four files: never apply a caller-supplied password
before settlement (store a bcrypt hash on the pending order and create
the login with `password_hash` when the payment settles; supabase-js
2.110 supports it), gate intake on `status = paid`, and route every
ownership check through one helper that escapes the pattern.

### 1.3 Old invoice pay links still charge, and a payment on one is invisible (verified)

The seven legacy invoices were billed through throwaway products on our
checkout. Those products are still active for all six non-void invoices,
including the five already paid, so a client with an old invoice email can
pay it again. And when someone does pay on an old link, checkout flips the
order but never stamps the invoice (`lib/checkout/settle.ts:61-121`), so
the invoice reads open here, open in the portal, and open in HighLevel,
which never saw the money. INV-1012 ($11,000, HighLevel Inc.) and
INV-1010 ($441) are the two open ones.

Fix: deactivate the products behind the five paid invoices now (a data
change), and make settle stamp the invoice paid and record the payment on
its HighLevel copy when an invoice product is paid.

### 1.4 Two sync workers can create the same HighLevel invoice or schedule twice (verified)

The outbox drain reads pending rows and marks them done only after the
HighLevel calls; nothing claims a row first (`lib/highlevel/sync.ts:801`).
The nightly reconcile at 04:00 also drains, in the same second as the
minute cron, so two workers overlap every night; a hand run overlaps
too. Two workers on one unlinked row both create: two invoices, two
started retainer schedules (a partner billed twice a month), two deal
cards. Three related faults in the same module:

- Recording a premade sale writes our side last (`lib/highlevel/money.ts`,
  `syncOrderSale`): a failure after the invoice is created retries and
  creates another, and the pull then imports the orphan as an open bill.
- A retainer schedule that fails to start is never retried, because the
  contact's fingerprint was written before it (`sync.ts:296-311`).
- Edits made inside HighLevel beyond the newest 100 are dropped: one page,
  no paging (`lib/highlevel/inbound.ts:134`).

Fix: lease a row before processing (a conditional update on
`next_attempt_at`), write our id right after each create, fold the
schedule into the fingerprint, page the contact poll.

## 2. Fix soon

Money and Stripe:

- A retry of finalize can rewrite an order that is already paid, adding
  bumps or a capped coupon; the webhook flags the mismatch but still sends
  the paid emails and creates the videos (`finalize/route.ts:171-214`).
- A late `customer.subscription.updated` after a `deleted` resurrects a
  cancelled plan: MRR counted, credits keep opening
  (`app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts:482`).
- A paid editing credit pack expands to a phantom video row
  (`lib/deliverables.ts:74-121`). No such order exists yet.
- A refund never reaches HighLevel's ledger; the paid invoice recorded
  there stays paid (`stripe/route.ts:365`, `admin/orders/[id]/refund`).
- Void has no paid guard (`admin/invoices/[id]/route.ts:116`); partial
  payments and lost disputes are invisible; the dashboard and the sales
  screen define "this month" differently.

HighLevel mirror:

- The reconcile never covers orders (the table has no `updated_at`; the
  query error is swallowed) and only partly covers videos and invoices.
- A client who is also a partner loses the partner tag on every sync.
- The portal thread pulls every outbound HighLevel email, including the
  transactional ones the platform itself sends through HighLevel, back
  into the client's Messages as studio chat bubbles (`conversations.ts:211`).
- Permanent errors (a 422) retry four times a day forever; the only alarm
  is nightly. The invoice pull re-examines the sub-account's other 100
  invoices every minute.
- The two demo invoices now trip the nightly "invoice not in HighLevel"
  check every night (they are skipped by design; the check should skip
  them too).

Email, bells, preferences:

- Custom-project emails link to `/portal/custom/`, which lands on the
  dashboard; the bell is right, the email is not (`lib/email/notify.ts:824`).
  The nine August bells flagged nightly point at "custom" and should be
  repaired to "projects".
- Order updates bypass HighLevel (no template key) and land on Brevo.
- The reminders carry no preference category, so a client who turned off
  "Progress on my videos" still gets them; no email carries a settings link.
- An invoice paid inside HighLevel tells nobody: no receipt, no bell.
- The ledger and the sweep's reads are capped at 1,000 rows with no paging.
- A burst of sends over HighLevel's rate limit silently falls back to
  Brevo, off the client's thread.

Screens (portal and admin):

- The Journal's note box is declared inside its screen component, so every
  keystroke remounts it (`app/admin/JournalScreen.tsx:265`): the caret
  jumps and typing breaks, the same class of bug as the modal that lost
  focus in August.
- A Sales Rep sees company revenue: the dashboard is allowed for every
  role and renders all-time revenue, MRR, owed and pipeline with no role
  check (`app/admin/roles.ts:109`, `app/api/admin/dashboard/route.ts:29`).
  More broadly, the menu grants gate the menu only: 60 of 64 admin API
  routes check the allowlist, not the role, so a Sales Rep's token can
  read sales and subscription detail. A policy call, flagged.
- The bell for a new editing file points at "editing", which is not a
  portal section, so the click does nothing (`lib/project-files.ts:278`).
- A teammate with an explicit grant loses the Custom line on the dashboard
  (`app/portal/DashboardView.tsx:436` checks a key that no grant carries).
- Date-only columns (invoice due date, quote valid-until, decision date)
  print the previous day for a US reader, because a bare date is parsed
  as UTC midnight (`app/portal/PortalClient.tsx:975` and three siblings).
- The customer portal prints "$445.5" for a partner-discounted price
  (five money formatters with no maximum digits); admin already fixed it.
- Smaller: the sidebar items remount on every badge poll and drop
  keyboard focus; nearly every admin and portal API call pays a redirect
  round trip (fetches without the trailing slash); the demo account's
  jobs count in "In the studio"; two inputs have no visible focus; the
  Messages screen has no error state.

Configuration and operations:

- The migrations bookkeeping table was created outside the migrations, so
  it had no row security and the public browser key could insert or delete
  its rows (verified on staging; production came from the same script).
  Locked on both projects on 15 September; the bootstrap now does it.
- The README and the env example document the Stripe and HighLevel
  webhook URLs without the trailing slash and on the apex domain, the same
  redirect trap the crons fell into. The live Stripe endpoint is right; the
  docs are not.
- The journal script writes to staging unless told otherwise, and the
  working guide's commands do not tell it, so entries can land where the
  next refresh erases them. Sixteen were carried across by hand at go-live.
- The local production env carried a localhost site URL, so the first fill
  mirrored localhost links into HighLevel until the minute cron re-sent
  them from Vercel with the right origin.
- Cron failures are invisible: only the price-drift cron catches and
  alarms; the others throw to a 500 in Vercel's logs. A slow HighLevel can
  kill the minute sync mid-way with Health still green until the nightly
  count.
- Every browser API call pays a redirect round trip (about 130 fetches
  without the trailing slash); `NEXT_PUBLIC_HL_LOCATION` and `MAINTENANCE`
  are read but undocumented; the lead pipeline ids fall back to hard-coded
  values with only a console warning; quotes and invoices share one number
  sequence; CLAUDE.md and README carry stale counts and lists.
- No unit test covers settlement, the Stripe webhook, the alarm throttle,
  the cron auth paths or the email fallback; the walkthroughs need a live
  server and cannot run in CI.

Data on production:

- One approved video (0f73ef7a) has no cut to watch.
- One client login has never signed in.

## 3. Low

- A spoofed crawler user agent skips the region and VPN gate everywhere,

## 4. Checked and found sound

Every admin route verifies the admin and the team routes check the role;
every portal route resolves the acting account and answers 404 on another
client's id; partner routes require an active partner. The Stripe webhook
checks the signature over the raw body, records the event once, flips paid
once, verifies the amount and currency, recovers orphan intents and holds
fulfilment on a mismatch. The service role key never reaches the browser.
Share links are random or UUID tokens, all noindex. Rate limits stand on
the quote form, checkout, chat, intake and the partner application.

Price truth holds: every code price and bundle matches the database, every
pack expands to its advertised count. Paid is one column everywhere
(`paid_at`), read the same way by the admin list, the portal, the public
invoice page, the dashboard, sales and lifetime value.

Screens: the phase 6 revert is clean (no trace of the removed views or
components); every portal link, bell and email link names a real section
except the two noted; every admin link names a real view; no copy-rule
breaks (dashes, retired CTAs, old counts) in portal, admin or partner
strings; no cross-surface imports; every table sits in a scrolling
wrapper; every add and edit form opens in the Modal, which traps focus and
closes on Escape; portal visibility only ever narrows.

Live: all 27 public pages answer 200 with a canonical; sitemap, robots and
the 404 page work; the Stripe webhook points at the slashed www URL with
all seven events; nine payments in 45 days match nine paid orders; every
table on the new database matches the old one; the outbox is empty; all 12
customers, 13 projects (13 deal cards, 13 records), 111 videos, 11
partners, 3 leads and 6 invoices are linked to HighLevel; the minute cron
fires on its own; 14 of 17 nightly facts hold and the three warnings are
explained above. Every admin and portal screen renders without an error of
our own.

## 5. Decisions, taken on 15 September

1. **The sweep tomorrow**: fix tonight, bounded to the last fourteen days.
2. **The login hole**: fix tonight (the password moves to settlement, the
   intake page needs a paid order, the ownership helper escapes patterns).
3. **The old invoice links**: only the portal's HighLevel link works from
   now on; every legacy invoice product is switched off.
4. **The sync hardening**: the lease, the write order, the schedule
   fingerprint and the contact paging, in one change with its tests.

Everything under "Fix soon" and "Low" waits for a normal working day.
